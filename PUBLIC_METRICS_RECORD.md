## Deliberately excluded

No country/client reach number is presented because the supplied Fiverr country figures do not form a validated unified reporting set, and no source export or date range has been provided. The `$10k+ earnings` statement is also not exposed on the public Fiverr profile. The site should only add a `20+ countries` or earnings figure after a dated Fiverr analytics export or dashboard screenshot confirms the reporting period and calculation. Those records can be cited as a seller-provided primary record rather than misrepresented as a public Fiverr statistic.
